Why hand-dyed

It photographs

A raw silk edge reads as texture in a close-up. A synthetic edge reads as plastic, and every bouquet detail shot finds it.

Silk also moves. It falls and catches air in a way polyester never manages, which matters in every photograph taken while someone is walking.

It matches

Catalogue ribbon comes in the colours a mill decided to make. Hand dyeing starts from your palette, so the ribbon can sit half a shade under the blush rather than fighting it.

Small batches mean a studio can order for one wedding instead of a warehouse.
