Our Philosophy

Florals that feel like they grew here

We believe the most beautiful arrangements don't look arranged at all. Every stem is placed with care, but the result should feel effortless — as if the flowers simply decided to bloom right there, in that moment, for you.

Garden roses layered with ranunculus, trailing jasmine, textured eucalyptus. We design with the seasons and let nature lead. Each bloom is chosen not just for beauty, but for the emotion it carries — a language of flowers that has been spoken since the Victorian era.

Meet Kate

A family-led floral studio rooted in love

Kate Jones founded Juniper Floral Studio with a simple belief: flowers should make you feel something. Every wedding, every event, every single stem is an opportunity to create wonder.

A lifelong student of the Victorian language of flowers, Kate designs with botanical intention — choosing each bloom not just for its beauty, but for the emotion it carries. Peonies for romance, ranunculus for charm, garden roses for passion. Every arrangement tells a story only flowers can speak.

What started as a passion project has grown into a family affair — supported by her husband Jordan and her parents, who help bring each vision to life with warmth, dedication, and a whole lot of early mornings at the flower market.
