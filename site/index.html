About

Meet Kate

Juniper Floral Studio was born from a love of gardens, art, and the belief that flowers have the power to transform a space. Kate brings warmth, creativity, and a natural eye for design to every arrangement she creates.

As a family-run studio, we pour our hearts into every wedding and event. Kate founded Juniper with the vision of creating garden-style floral design that feels lush, organic, and deeply personal — as though each arrangement was just gathered from a sun-dappled meadow on a perfect morning.

Every design begins with a conversation, because every love story is different. We believe that flowers should do more than decorate — they should move you, surprise you, and make you feel something.

After years of designing for celebrations in Toronto and Southern Ontario, Juniper Floral Studio is beginning a new chapter in Austin, bringing the same romantic garden sensibility to weddings across the Hill Country and Central Texas.
