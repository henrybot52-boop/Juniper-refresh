About

Kate Jones, lead designer

Toronto-rooted and now based in Austin, Juniper Floral Studio is guided by romance, artistry, and a love of gardens at their peak.

Our story

Designing wonder, delight, and romance

Kate Jones is the owner and lead designer of Juniper Floral Studio. She specializes in bridal bouquets, handwoven garlands, and bespoke floral design. She's passionate about bringing wonder, delight and romance to weddings. Juniper is a family affair — Kate's husband Jordan helps with all things business, and her recently retired parents work for Juniper too.

Each design blends seasonal blooms with intentional color stories. Signature ingredients include vernaculus, petraeus, ranunculus, seeded eucalyptus, and garden rose to create lush, natural movement.

Design ethos

Garden-first compositions

Designs feel naturally gathered, layered with movement, and rich in texture for an immersive look.

High-touch planning

Toronto to Austin
