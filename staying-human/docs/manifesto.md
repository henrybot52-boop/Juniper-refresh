# The Staying Human Manifesto

1. **Some things should be inefficient.** The point of baking bread with your
   kid is not the bread.

2. **Presence is the product.** A tradition transmitted by video is a
   recording. A tradition transmitted across a kitchen table is alive.

3. **Hands remember what clouds forget.** Skills stored in bodies —
   kneading, whittling, harmonizing, dealing cards — survive every platform
   migration.

4. **Stories want tellers, not just storage.** An archived story is a seed.
   Only a human telling makes it grow.

5. **Slow is a feature.** Handwritten letters arrive late and are kept for
   decades. Optimize for the decades.

6. **Machines can assist; they may not replace the circle.** Use AI to find
   the recipe, translate the lullaby, print the invitations. Then put the
   phone down and show up.

7. **Every generation is a bottleneck.** A tradition skips one generation
   and it's gone. The work is now, always.

8. **Joy is the strategy.** People don't preserve what they're guilted
   into. They preserve what they love doing together.
