# Mission

Staying Human exists to keep human traditions alive, practiced, and passed on
as artificial intelligence transforms everything else.

## Why now

AI can write, paint, compose, converse, and soon may do most economic work.
That changes what humans *have* to do — but not what makes life feel like
ours. Traditions are how humans have always encoded meaning: in bread, in
song, in stories told badly and lovingly, in the way a holiday smells.

When everything can be generated, the *ungenerated* becomes precious:
the thing your grandmother actually made, the song your friends actually
sang, the letter someone actually wrote to you.

## What we do

1. **Preserve** — collect and document traditions before they fade,
   in the words of the people who keep them.
2. **Practice** — run workshops, gatherings, and seasonal events where
   people do traditions together, in person, with their hands.
3. **Pass on** — pair tradition keepers with learners so knowledge moves
   between generations the way it always has: person to person.

## What we are not

- We are not anti-technology. We use tools; we just don't outsource meaning.
- We are not a museum. Traditions here are practiced, not embalmed.
- We are not nostalgia. This is about the future — a future with humans
  recognizably in it.
