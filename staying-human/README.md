# Staying Human

**Keeping human traditions alive in the age of AI and the singularity.**

The faster machines get, the more the slow things matter. Staying Human is a
company — and a community — devoted to preserving, practicing, and passing on
the traditions that make us who we are: cooking together, telling stories,
making things by hand, singing, playing, writing letters, gathering around
tables and fires.

We are not against technology. We just believe some things should stay in
human hands.

## The eight pillars

1. **Food & Cooking** — recipes with lineage, cooked by hand
2. **Storytelling** — spoken stories, oral history, family lore
3. **Music & Song** — singing together, learned by ear
4. **Crafts & Making** — things shaped by human hands
5. **Rituals & Celebrations** — holidays, rites of passage, seasons
6. **Games & Play** — play that needs people in a room
7. **Letters & Correspondence** — words written slowly, on purpose
8. **Gatherings & Community** — the table, the fire, the front porch

## Running the website

The site is intentionally framework-free. Open it directly:

```bash
open website/index.html        # macOS
# or serve it:
cd website && python3 -m http.server 8000
```
