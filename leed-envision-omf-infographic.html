Where LEED and Envision Overlap

Operations & maintenance sustainability credits where a single analysis or data stream earns recognition in both systems — LEED v4.1 O+M (the OMF building) and Envision v3 (the transit infrastructure). Do the work once; report it twice.

LEED v4.1 O+M — measured building performance
Shared evidence — one analysis feeds both
Envision v3 — infrastructure plan & commitment
Scoring differs: Envision credits the plan/commitment for long-term monitoring; LEED O+M credits the measured result. The same meters & systems supply both.

⤳ Highest-value "do-once" items

  • One Sustainability Management Plan — Envision LD2.1 + anchors LEED integrative/innovation docs.
  • One metering / BAS backbone — energy + water submetering feeds LEED EA/WE performance and Envision RA2.4 / RA3.4 monitoring.
  • One commute survey + multimodal inventory — and an OMF is a transit facility, so the transportation story is strong on both sides.
  • One waste-diversion tracking system — LEED MR + Envision RA1.3 / RA1.4.
  • One climate-risk & resilience assessment — Envision CR2.x + LEED resilience credits.

◎ Austin-specific leverage

  • Austin Energy GreenChoice renewable procurement & rebates feed RA2.3 / EA Renewable Energy.
  • Strong solar resource makes on-site PV credible for both energy categories.
  • Heat & water pay off twice: heat-island + cooling-water efficiency + extreme-heat resilience; drought + water reduction.
  • Grid & flood resilience — post-Winter-Storm-Uri grid hardening and flash-flood stormwater design support Envision CR2.x.
  • If Austin Energy Green Building (AEGB) is also pursued, the same documents extend to a third system.
Reuse the analysis, not the forms. Each system has its own baselines, thresholds and submittal templates — so budget effort for reformatting evidence to each, not for re-doing the engineering. Confirm registered versions; credit IDs are version-specific (LEED v4 ≠ v4.1; Envision v2 ≠ v3).
