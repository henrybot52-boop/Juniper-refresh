# Community

## Contributing a tradition

Add a markdown file to the matching pillar under [`traditions/`](../traditions/)
using this template:

```markdown
# <Name of the tradition>

**Pillar:** Food & Cooking
**Lineage:** e.g. "My grandmother, Oaxaca, since at least the 1940s"
**Contributed by:** your name (optional)

## What it is
A short description in your own words.

## How to keep it
Step-by-step, with the details that matter — enough that a stranger
could practice it and get it *right*, not just done.

## Why it matters
What this tradition holds. Who it connects. What would be lost.
```

## Guidelines

- **Write it human.** First person is welcome. Imperfect is welcome.
- **Honor lineage.** Credit where a tradition comes from; don't claim
  what isn't yours to claim.
- **Practice over polish.** A rough entry that gets practiced beats a
  perfect entry that doesn't.
- **AI assist is fine; AI authorship isn't.** Use tools to translate or
  transcribe, but the tradition itself must come from a person who keeps it.
